Code of Conduct
Our Pledge

We are committed to creating an open, friendly, and welcoming environment for everyone contributing to Unnamed-DE.

Unnamed-DE is a small project built by young developers. We believe that everyone, regardless of experience or skill level, deserves an opportunity to learn, contribute, experiment, and grow together.

We are committed to maintaining a community where people can collaborate freely while treating one another with respect.

Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior include:

Being respectful and considerate toward everyone.
Welcoming beginners and first-time contributors.
Giving constructive feedback and accepting suggestions.
Respecting different ideas, opinions, and coding styles.
Helping others learn and improve their skills.
Being patient when reviewing code or discussing problems.
Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

Harassment, bullying, or discrimination.
Personal attacks, insults, or humiliating comments.
Trolling or deliberately disrupting discussions.
Publishing someone's private information without permission.
Mocking others because of their experience or skill level.
Deliberately submitting malicious or harmful contributions.
Any behavior that makes the community unsafe or unwelcoming.
Our Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for maintaining and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions, comments, issues, and pull requests that violate these standards.

They may also take appropriate action against individuals who repeatedly engage in unacceptable behavior.

Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all spaces associated with Unnamed-DE, including:

GitHub repositories.
Issues and pull requests.
Discussions and code reviews.
Project-related communication channels.
Other spaces where someone represents the project.
Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please contact the project maintainers privately through GitHub.

Please provide relevant details about the incident so that maintainers can review the situation appropriately.

All reports will be handled with care and discretion.

Enforcement

Depending on the severity of the violation, maintainers may take the following actions:

A friendly reminder or warning.
A formal warning.
Temporary restriction from project participation.
Permanent removal from the project community.

Enforcement decisions will be made fairly and proportionately.

Our Commitment

We understand that everyone is still learning and growing.

Mistakes can happen, and we encourage people to learn from them. However, respect, responsibility, and kindness should always remain fundamental values of this community.

Let's build, learn, and improve together.
